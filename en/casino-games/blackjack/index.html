Blackjack is a comparing-card game where the player tries to finish with a stronger hand than the dealer without exceeding 21. Unlike many casino games, player decisions can influence the mathematical result of a hand.

The objective of blackjack

The goal is to beat the dealer by having a valid hand closer to 21 than the dealer, or by remaining in the hand when the dealer busts.

A natural blackjack is usually an Ace plus a ten-value card in the first two cards, but payout rules vary by table.

Hit, stand, double and split

Hit

Take another card.

Stand

Keep the current total and end your decisions for the hand.

Double down

Increase the wager under the table rules and receive a limited additional card, commonly one.

Split

When permitted, separate a pair into two hands and place an additional wager.

Dealer rules and table variations

The dealer follows fixed drawing rules, such as hitting to a defined total and standing on another. Tables can differ on soft 17, doubling, splitting, number of decks and blackjack payout. These differences can change the house edge.

Check whether blackjack pays 3:2 or 6:5

A lower blackjack payout generally makes the game less favourable to the player, all else being equal.

Basic strategy versus prediction

Blackjack basic strategy is a mathematically derived decision framework based on the player hand and dealer up-card. It can reduce avoidable decision errors under a defined ruleset, but it cannot guarantee a win or eliminate the house edge.

Side bets

Live and digital blackjack tables may add side bets such as pairs or three-card combinations. These use separate payout tables and often have a different, sometimes substantially higher, house edge than the main blackjack hand.

How to evaluate a blackjack table professionally

“Blackjack” is not one fixed ruleset. Small rule changes can materially alter the mathematical house edge and the correct basic-strategy decisions.

1

Check the natural-blackjack payout: 3:2 and 6:5 are not equivalent.

2

Check dealer soft 17: whether the dealer hits or stands changes the ruleset and strategy.

3

Check splitting, doubling and surrender: these options can change both strategy and house edge.

4

Separate the main hand from side bets: side bets use their own paytables and can carry a very different house edge.

Frequently asked questions

What is a blackjack?

A blackjack is commonly an Ace plus any ten-value card as the initial two-card hand, subject to the table’s specific rules.

Does the dealer choose whether to hit or stand?

No. The dealer follows fixed drawing rules defined by the game.

Can blackjack strategy guarantee profit?

No. Basic strategy can improve decisions for a particular ruleset but does not remove randomness or the house edge.

Why do blackjack payout rules matter?

A table that pays less for a natural blackjack generally increases the house advantage compared with an otherwise similar table.
