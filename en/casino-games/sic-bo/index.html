Sic Bo is a three-dice casino game. Players bet on totals, number combinations, doubles, triples and other outcomes before the dice are rolled or shaken. Different bets have very different probabilities and payout structures.

How Sic Bo works

Three dice determine the result of each round. Players choose from a betting layout before betting closes, then the dice outcome is compared with each selected bet.

Sic Bo is sometimes called Tai Sai, Big Small or related regional names, although table rules can vary by market and provider.

Small and Big bets

S

Small

Commonly covers totals 4–10, except when the dice form a triple.

B

Big

Commonly covers totals 11–17, except when the dice form a triple.

Always check the actual table rules because variants may handle special outcomes differently.

Other common Sic Bo bets

Specific total

Bet on the exact sum of the three dice.

Double

Bet that a selected pair value appears.

Triple

Bet on a specific triple or on any triple, depending on the table.

Single number

Bet that a chosen number appears on one or more dice, often with tiered payouts.

Combination

Bet that two selected numbers both appear.

Payouts and probability

Rare combinations such as specific triples can pay much more than common outcomes, but the probability is correspondingly lower. The house edge is not the same across all bets.

Do not judge a bet only by its payout

A very high payout usually signals that the outcome is much less likely to occur.

Live and digital Sic Bo

Sic Bo can be delivered as an RNG game or as a live dealer game using physical dice and studio equipment. Some providers also add enhanced variants with multipliers or bonus-style mechanics.

Common dice-game misconceptions

Previous dice totals do not make a future total “due.” Even when several rounds show similar results, a properly random next round is not guaranteed to balance the history.

Why Sic Bo bets are not equally priced

All three dice are part of the same round, but different bets cover very different numbers of possible combinations. That is why a high advertised payout does not automatically mean better value.

216 possible ordered outcomes

Three six-sided dice produce 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 ordered combinations. Sic Bo probabilities are built from how many of those combinations satisfy each bet.

Frequently asked questions

How many dice are used in Sic Bo?

Standard Sic Bo uses three dice.

What do Small and Big mean?

They are broad total bets, commonly Small 4–10 and Big 11–17, usually excluding triples under standard rules.

Do all Sic Bo bets have the same house edge?

No. Different bet types have different probabilities, payouts and mathematical house edges.

Can past dice results predict the next round?

No. A properly random dice outcome is not guaranteed by recent totals or streaks.
