Roulette is a wheel game where players bet on where a ball will settle. Bets can cover a single number, groups of numbers or broader properties such as red/black and odd/even.

How the roulette wheel works

A roulette wheel contains numbered pockets. The dealer or automated game spins the wheel and ball, and the winning number is the pocket where the ball comes to rest.
0

European roulette

Commonly uses numbers 1–36 plus a single zero.

00

American roulette

Commonly adds both 0 and 00, changing the probabilities and house edge.

Inside bets

Inside bets are placed on the numbered area of the layout and normally cover smaller groups of numbers.

Straight up

One number.

Split

Two adjacent numbers.

Street

A row of three numbers.

Corner

Four numbers meeting at one corner.

Outside bets

Outside bets cover larger groups such as red/black, odd/even, high/low, dozens and columns. They usually pay less than single-number bets because more outcomes are covered.

Payouts and probability

A larger advertised payout generally corresponds to a lower probability of hitting that bet. For example, a straight-up number has a much larger payout than red/black, but covers only one numbered pocket.

The zero creates the house edge

Even-money bets do not cover the zero pocket, which is why a single-zero roulette wheel still has a mathematical house advantage.

Betting systems and streaks

Systems that change stake size after wins or losses cannot change the underlying probability of the wheel. They mainly change the pattern and size of wagers, and progressive systems can increase exposure quickly.

Live versus RNG roulette

RNG roulette generates digital outcomes through approved game software, while live roulette uses a physical wheel streamed from a studio or casino environment. Rules and bet types should still be checked at the individual table.

European vs American roulette

The extra double-zero pocket is the most important structural difference between the two common roulette wheel types.

FeatureEuropean rouletteAmerican roulette
Numbered pockets37 (1–36 plus 0)38 (1–36 plus 0 and 00)
Standard single-zero house edgeAbout 2.70%About 5.26% on standard bets
Even-money betsRed/black, odd/even, 1–18/19–36Same categories, but both 0 and 00 fall outside them
What to checkWhether La Partage or En Prison appliesWhether the wheel is standard double-zero or a special variant

Wheel type matters more than a betting pattern

A staking system changes how much is wagered after wins or losses; it does not remove the underlying mathematical effect of the zero pockets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between European and American roulette?

European roulette usually has one zero, while American roulette commonly has both 0 and 00.

Is red or black a 50/50 bet?

No. The zero, and double zero on American wheels, means red and black each cover fewer than half of all pockets.

Can a roulette system overcome the house edge?

Changing stake patterns does not change the mathematical probability built into the wheel and payout table.

Do previous roulette spins affect the next spin?

No. A properly conducted spin is not made predictable by the previous results displayed on the history board.
