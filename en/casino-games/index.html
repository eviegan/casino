What should beginners know about casino games?

Rules come before strategy

Every game has its own rules, payout structure and probability model. Before placing a bet, understand what counts as a win, what each wager costs and how payouts are calculated.

RTP and house edge are long-run measures

Return to Player and house edge describe theoretical performance over many rounds. They do not guarantee a specific result during one session, and previous outcomes do not make a future result “due”.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What are the main types of casino games?

Common categories include slots, table games such as blackjack and roulette, live dealer games, multiplier games such as crash, poker variants, fishing-style games and dice games such as Sic Bo.

What is RTP?

RTP, or Return to Player, is a theoretical long-run percentage used to describe how much a game returns across a very large number of rounds. It is not a promise for an individual session.

What is house edge?

House edge is the mathematical advantage built into a game or specific bet. Different bets in the same game can have different house edges.

Can previous casino game results predict the next round?

No. Properly random or independently determined outcomes are not made predictable by recent wins, losses or streaks.
