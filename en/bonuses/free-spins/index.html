Free spins are promotional slot rounds provided under specific terms. The important details are not just how many spins you receive, but where they can be used, what each spin is worth and how any winnings are treated.

What are free spins?

Free spins are promotional rounds that can be used on specified slot games without placing the normal cash wager for each round.

The promotion may be part of a welcome offer, reload bonus, loyalty reward or standalone campaign.

Why spin value matters

50

Number of spins

The headline may advertise 20, 50, 100 or more spins.

R$

Value per spin

Each promotional spin may use a fixed stake. More spins do not necessarily mean more total promotional value.

Compare total value, not only quantity

50 spins at R$0.20 each represent a different promotional value from 50 spins at R$1.00 each.

Which games can free spins be used on?

Most free-spin promotions are restricted to one game or a small list of eligible slots. The game selection can matter because RTP, volatility and features differ between titles.

1G

Single-game offer

All spins must be used on one named slot.

MG

Multiple-game offer

The user may choose from a limited eligible game list.

What happens to free-spin winnings?

Winnings may be added as cash, bonus funds or a restricted promotional balance. Some offers apply wagering requirements or a maximum conversion amount.

“Free spin winnings” can have separate rules

Always check whether winnings are immediately withdrawable or subject to additional conditions.

Expiry and activation rules

A

Activation deadline

The spins may need to be claimed within a limited period.

U

Usage deadline

Unused spins can expire after a stated number of hours or days.

W

Winnings deadline

Promotional winnings may have a separate period for completing wagering.

How should beginners compare free-spin offers?

Number of spins

How many promotional rounds are included.

Value per spin

The stake amount used for each free spin.

Eligible game

The slot or list of slots where the spins can be used.

Winnings rules

Whether winnings are cash, bonus funds or subject to wagering.

Expiry

How long you have to activate and use the spins.

Frequently asked questions

Are free spins completely free?

They are promotional rounds, but the winnings or usage can still be subject to eligibility, game restrictions, expiry or wagering rules.

Can I choose any slot?

Usually not. Most free-spin offers specify one game or a limited eligible list.

Do free-spin winnings need wagering?

Sometimes. The exact treatment depends on the promotion terms.

Is 100 free spins always better than 50?

No. Spin value, eligible games, winnings rules and expiry can make a smaller offer more useful.
