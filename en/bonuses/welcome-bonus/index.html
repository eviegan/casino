A welcome bonus is a promotion offered to eligible new users, usually after registration or a qualifying first deposit. The headline percentage is only one part of the offer — the terms decide how useful it really is.

What is a welcome bonus?

A welcome bonus is a promotional offer for eligible new users. It may add bonus funds, free spins or another benefit after registration or a qualifying deposit.

The headline offer can look simple, but the actual value depends on the conditions attached to it.

Common welcome-bonus formats

%

Deposit match

The operator adds bonus funds based on a percentage of an eligible deposit, up to a stated limit.

FS

Free spins

A set number of promotional slot rounds may be included, often on selected games.

PK

Welcome package

The promotion may be split across multiple deposits or stages rather than one single offer.

Why wagering requirements matter

Bonus-related funds may need to be played through a stated number of times before withdrawal is allowed. This can apply to the bonus only, or to the deposit plus bonus, depending on the terms.

Example

If a R$100 bonus has a 20× wagering requirement on the bonus amount, the required play-through would be R$2,000 before the bonus balance becomes eligible under those terms.

Important limits to check

MAX

Maximum bonus

The deposit match normally stops at a stated maximum amount.

BET

Maximum bet

Many promotions restrict how much can be wagered per round while bonus terms are active.

G

Eligible games

Some games may contribute fully, partially or not at all toward wagering.

WD

Withdrawal conditions

Bonus funds, winnings or promotional balances may have specific cash-out rules.

Expiry dates and eligibility

Promotions can have a limited activation window and a separate deadline for completing wagering. Eligibility can also depend on age, location, account status, payment method or whether the user has already claimed a previous offer.

Do not rely on the banner alone

The full terms should explain who qualifies, how long the offer lasts and what happens when it expires.

What should beginners check first?

Match percentage

How much bonus value is added to a qualifying deposit.

Maximum bonus

The largest promotional amount available.

Wagering requirement

The amount that must be played through before withdrawal eligibility.

Maximum bet

The largest allowed wager while the bonus is active.

Expiry

The deadline for claiming and completing the offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is a welcome bonus free money?

No. Welcome bonuses normally include eligibility rules, wagering requirements, game restrictions, limits and expiry conditions.

What does a 100% deposit match mean?

It usually means the operator matches an eligible deposit by the same amount up to a stated maximum, subject to the offer terms.

Can I withdraw a bonus immediately?

Usually not if wagering or other promotional conditions have not been completed.

Do all games count toward wagering?

No. Game contribution can vary and some games may be excluded.
