How Wagering Works

Wagering requirements are one of the most important parts of a casino bonus. They determine how much eligible play must be completed before promotional funds or winnings can become withdrawable.

Wagering requirements are one of the most important parts of a casino bonus. They determine how much eligible play must be completed before promotional funds or winnings can become withdrawable.

What is a wagering requirement?

A wagering requirement is the amount of eligible play-through required before bonus-related funds become withdrawable under the promotion rules.

It is often written as a multiplier such as 10×, 20×, 30× or 40×.

What amount is the multiplier applied to?

Bonus only

A 20× requirement on a R$100 bonus means R$2,000 of eligible wagering.

Bonus + deposit

A 20× requirement on a R$100 deposit plus R$100 bonus means R$4,000 of eligible wagering.

Always identify the calculation base

Two bonuses with the same “20×” headline can require very different total wagering.

Worked examples

R$50 bonus

10× bonus only = R$500 required wagering.

R$100 bonus

20× bonus only = R$2,000 required wagering.

R$100 + R$100

30× deposit + bonus = R$6,000 required wagering.

What is game contribution?

Not every game necessarily counts 100% toward wagering. Slots may contribute fully while table games can contribute less or be excluded entirely.

100% contribution

Every R$1 wagered counts as R$1 toward the requirement.

20% contribution

Every R$1 wagered counts as only R$0.20 toward the requirement.

0% contribution

The wager does not reduce the requirement.

Maximum bet rules

Many bonus terms limit the largest wager allowed while wagering is incomplete. Breaking this rule can affect bonus eligibility or promotional winnings.

Check the maximum before playing

Do not assume your normal stake is permitted while a bonus is active.

Common wagering mistakes

Reading only the multiplier

Users forget to check whether it applies to the bonus only or deposit + bonus.

Ignoring game contribution

A low-contribution game can make the requirement take much longer.

Breaking max-bet rules

Oversized wagers can violate promotion conditions.

Missing expiry

Incomplete wagering can expire before the user finishes it.

How to read wagering requirements professionally

The multiplier alone is not enough. A complete interpretation needs the calculation base, contribution rules, maximum-bet conditions and deadline.

1

Identify the base: bonus only, deposit + bonus, winnings, or another stated amount.

2

Convert the multiplier into currency: calculate the actual amount of eligible wagering required.

3

Adjust for game contribution: a 20% contribution means R$100 of play may reduce the requirement by only R$20.

4

Check restrictions and expiry: maximum stakes and completion deadlines can materially affect whether a requirement is realistic.

Current UK regulatory example

For Great Britain–licensed operators, a rule effective 19 January 2026 limits wagering requirements attached to incentives to a maximum of 10× bonus funds. This is jurisdiction-specific and should not be assumed to apply elsewhere.

Wagering calculation

If a R$100 bonus carries a 20× bonus-only wagering requirement, the stated wagering target is R$2,000 before accounting for eligible-game contribution and other conditions.

Check your understanding: Always identify whether the multiplier applies to bonus funds only or deposit plus bonus.

Frequently asked questions

What does 20× wagering mean?

It means the stated calculation base must be wagered twenty times before the promotion conditions are completed.

Does wagering always apply to the bonus only?

No. Some offers apply it to the bonus only, while others use deposit plus bonus.

Do all games count 100%?

No. Game contribution can vary and some games may contribute nothing.

Can I withdraw before wagering is complete?

The result depends on the promotion terms; requesting a withdrawal may cancel the bonus or make promotional funds ineligible.
