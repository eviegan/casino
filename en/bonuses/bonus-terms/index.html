Bonus Terms

The headline tells you what the promotion advertises. The terms tell you how it works. This guide focuses on the conditions most likely to change the real value or usability of a casino bonus.

What are casino bonus terms?

Bonus terms are the conditions attached to a promotion, including who qualifies, what must be wagered, which games count, how long the offer lasts and what restrictions apply.

Core bonus terms

Eligibility

Who can claim the promotion and any account, country, deposit or opt-in requirements.

Bonus cap

The maximum promotional value available even if the advertised percentage would calculate to more.

Game contribution

The percentage of an eligible wager that counts toward wagering progress.

Maximum bet

The highest stake permitted while the relevant bonus conditions apply.

Expiry

The time limit for claiming, using or completing the promotion.

Wagering language

20×

Wagering requirement

The amount of eligible play that must be completed before bonus-related funds or winnings become withdrawable under the promotion rules.

BASE

Calculation base

The amount to which the wagering multiplier applies—for example, bonus only or deposit plus bonus.

Same multiplier, different requirement

Always identify both the multiplier and its calculation base.

Restrictions, expiry and withdrawals

GAME

Eligible games

Some games may contribute fully, partially or not at all.

TIME

Time limit

Promotions can expire before wagering is completed.

WD

Withdrawal effect

Terms should explain what happens if a withdrawal is requested while a promotion remains active.

How to read bonus terms professionally

1

Start with significant conditions: eligibility, wagering, maximum bet, game restrictions and expiry.

2

Separate cash from restricted funds: understand which balance is withdrawable and which has conditions attached.

3

Check consequences: terms should explain what happens if a restriction is breached.

4

Save the terms: for a meaningful promotion, keep a copy or screenshot of the terms that applied when you opted in.

Frequently asked questions

What are casino bonus terms?

They are the conditions that explain eligibility, wagering, game contribution, maximum bets, expiry, withdrawals and other rules attached to a promotion.

What is game contribution?

It is the percentage of an eligible wager that counts toward completing a wagering requirement.

What does maximum bet mean in bonus terms?

It is the largest permitted stake while the relevant promotion or restricted bonus funds are active.

Why should bonus terms be read before opting in?

Because significant conditions can materially change the real value and usability of an offer.
