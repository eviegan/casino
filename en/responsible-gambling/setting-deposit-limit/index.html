Setting Deposit Limits

Understand the tool, recognise the warning signs and know what practical action can come next.

PLANCHECKACT

A deposit limit is most useful when it is chosen before gambling starts and based on money that is genuinely available for entertainment.

1. What a deposit limit actually controls

A deposit limit restricts how much money can be paid into a gambling account over a stated period. It is different from a loss limit: depositing money and losing money are not the same measurement.

2. Start with your real household budget

Calculate essential commitments first: housing, utilities, food, transport, debt repayments, savings and other obligations. Gambling should never be funded with money needed for essentials.

3. Choose a period you can understand

Daily limits can create tight short-term boundaries, while weekly or monthly limits may better match some budgeting cycles. The important point is understanding exactly when the limit resets and what transactions count.

4. Choose the amount before you are emotionally involved

A limit chosen after a loss can be influenced by the urge to recover money. Set the amount when calm, and avoid treating a recent win as justification for increasing future spending.

5. Be cautious about increasing limits

A useful safer-gambling system should not make it effortless to raise spending during a session. If you frequently want to increase a limit, consider whether the original boundary is telling you something important.

6. Combine deposit limits with other tools

Money limits work better as part of a wider plan that can include time limits, reality checks, account-history reviews, bank gambling blocks and breaks from gambling.

7. Review behaviour, not only the number

Staying under a deposit limit does not automatically mean gambling is harmless. Also consider time spent, stress, secrecy, chasing losses and whether gambling is affecting relationships or responsibilities.

Frequently asked questions

What is a gambling deposit limit?

It is a restriction on how much money can be deposited into a gambling account during a defined period.

Is a deposit limit the same as a loss limit?

No. A deposit measures money paid into the account, while a loss measure concerns gambling outcomes. Platforms may offer different financial-limit tools.

Should I increase my limit after a win?

A win does not make future gambling safer. Limits are most useful when based on an affordable entertainment budget rather than recent results.

What if I keep trying to increase my deposit limit?

Repeated urges to override a planned boundary can be a warning sign. Consider taking a break or using stronger safeguards.

Important: Responsible-gambling tools and self-exclusion rules vary by operator and jurisdiction. Use the current rules shown by the licensed operator or relevant regulator.
