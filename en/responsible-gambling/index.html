Why It Matters

Responsible gambling is about control, not prediction

No tool changes the mathematics

Deposit limits, breaks and budgeting do not make casino games profitable. They help control exposure to games that still involve chance and a house advantage.

Support is part of the system

Responsible gambling is not limited to self-control. Account tools, family support, independent organizations and self-exclusion can all be part of a stronger safety plan.

FAQ

Responsible gambling questions

What is responsible gambling?

It means keeping gambling within planned financial and time limits while understanding that outcomes are uncertain and losses are possible.

What is a deposit limit?

A deposit limit caps how much money can be added to an account during a defined period.

What is a reality check?

A reality check is a timed reminder designed to make session duration more visible.

What is self-exclusion?

Self-exclusion restricts access to gambling for a chosen period and is generally a stronger control than a short break.

When should someone consider getting help?

If gambling is affecting finances, relationships, work, sleep or emotional wellbeing, outside support can be useful.

Do gambling systems remove risk?

No. Betting systems do not remove the underlying uncertainty or house advantage of casino games.
