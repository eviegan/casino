Time Limits

Understand the tool, recognise the warning signs and know what practical action can come next.

PLANCHECKACT

Time limits create a stopping point before a gambling session begins, reducing reliance on in-the-moment decisions.

1. Why time boundaries matter

Fast game cycles, continuous availability and immersive interfaces can make it easy to lose track of time. Longer sessions can also increase fatigue and reduce the quality of decisions.

2. Set the stop time before starting

Choose a clear end time rather than deciding to stop only when you feel finished. A defined clock time is easier to follow than an open-ended promise to play for a little while.

3. Use device and platform reminders

Use platform session tools where available, plus a phone alarm or calendar reminder as an independent backup. A reminder works best when you have already decided what action to take when it appears.

4. Make breaks physically different

A real break means stepping away from the gambling interface. Stand up, leave the room, get water or do another activity rather than simply switching between games.

5. Watch for repeated extensions

Adding another ten minutes again and again can turn a time limit into a suggestion. Repeated extensions are useful information about whether the session is becoming difficult to stop.

6. Protect sleep and daily routines

Late-night gambling can interfere with sleep and may make it easier to continue when tired. Avoid allowing gambling to replace sleep, work, meals, exercise or time with other people.

7. When a time limit is not enough

If you repeatedly ignore alarms or return immediately after stopping, consider a longer timeout, self-exclusion or additional support rather than simply setting another timer.

Frequently asked questions

What is a gambling time limit?

It is a pre-planned boundary for how long you will gamble or when a session must end.

Are reality checks the same as time limits?

Not exactly. A reality check reminds you about elapsed play, while a time limit or stop time is a boundary you decide to follow.

What if I keep extending my session?

Repeated extensions can indicate that stopping is becoming difficult. A longer break or stronger gambling-control tool may be more appropriate.

Should gambling replace sleep or other responsibilities?

No. Gambling should not displace sleep, work, essential responsibilities or important relationships.
