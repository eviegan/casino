What is a Bonus Game?

A bonus game is a game feature that temporarily moves play into a different rule set, screen, mode or reward mechanic beyond the normal base-game spin.

For example, a set of Bonus symbols may open a pick screen, trigger Free Spins or start a separate respin-style feature. The individual game rules determine what happens next.

Common bonus-game formats

P

Pick Bonus

The player chooses from objects, cards or symbols that reveal prizes, multipliers or progress.

◉

Wheel Bonus

A wheel or selector determines a prize, multiplier or next stage.

H

Hold & Win

Special symbols lock while a limited number of respins attempt to add more symbols.

FS

Free Spins

A separate sequence of spins may add Wilds, multipliers, expanded reels or other mechanics.

How bonus games are triggered

There is no single universal trigger. A bonus game may begin through scatters, Bonus symbols, collected items, a random event or another game-specific mechanic.

Trigger styleTypical exampleWhat to check
Scatter trigger3+ or 4+ special symbolsRequired count and reel positions
Collection triggerFill a meter or collect itemsWhether progress persists between spins
Random triggerFeature activates independentlyWhether it can happen in base play, bonus play or both

Frequently asked questions

What is a bonus game in a slot?

A bonus game is an additional feature or round that uses rules or mechanics beyond the normal base-game spin.

How do bonus games start?

They can be triggered by Scatter or Bonus symbols, collection mechanics, random events or other title-specific rules.

Is a Free Spins round a bonus game?

It can be. Free Spins are one of the most common bonus features, especially when the free-spin mode changes the normal mechanics.

Does a bonus game guarantee a win?

No. A bonus feature can create additional outcomes or mechanics, but it does not guarantee a profit.
