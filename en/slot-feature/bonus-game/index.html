What Is a Bonus Game?

A bonus game is a game feature that temporarily moves play into a different rule set, screen, mode or reward mechanic beyond the normal base-game spin.

For example, landing the required Bonus symbols might open a separate pick screen. The player selects from several objects and the feature reveals prizes according to the game’s predefined rules.

Important: There is no universal rule shared by every slot. Always check the exact game’s paytable and information screen for trigger requirements, payouts and feature behaviour.

Common types and variations

P

Pick Bonus

The player chooses from objects, cards or symbols that reveal prizes, multipliers or feature progress.

◉

Wheel Bonus

A wheel or similar selector determines a prize, multiplier or next feature stage according to the game rules.

H

Hold & Win

Special symbols lock in place while a limited number of respins attempt to add more symbols or fill positions.

FS

Free Spins Round

A separate sequence of spins may include enhanced Wilds, multipliers, expanding reels or other changed mechanics.

L

Level / Stage Bonus

The bonus progresses through multiple stages, with different rules or rewards at each step.

?

Random Feature

Some games can activate a bonus independently of a standard symbol trigger, subject to the game’s design.

Frequently asked questions

What is a bonus game in a slot?

A bonus game is an additional feature or round that uses rules or mechanics beyond the normal base-game spin.

How do bonus games start?

They can be triggered by Scatter or Bonus symbols, collection mechanics, random events or other title-specific rules.

Is a Free Spins round a bonus game?

It can be. Free Spins are one of the most common bonus features, especially when the free-spin mode changes the normal game mechanics.

Does a bonus game guarantee a win?

No. A bonus feature can create additional outcomes or mechanics, but it does not guarantee a profit.

Are all slot bonus games the same?

No. Pick games, wheels, hold-and-win respins, Free Spins and multi-stage bonuses can work very differently.
