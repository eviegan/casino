Ways to Win Explained

A Ways to Win system awards eligible combinations based on matching symbols appearing on consecutive reels, rather than requiring them to follow a fixed payline path.
Important: Similar feature names can use different mathematics and trigger rules. The exact game information screen should always be treated as the final reference.

Frequently asked questions

What does Ways to Win mean?

It means winning combinations are based on matching symbols appearing across consecutive reels rather than on fixed paylines.

What does 243 Ways mean?

It describes a common number of possible reel-position combinations available for eligible matching symbols.

Is 4,096 Ways better than 243 Ways?

Not automatically. A larger ways count changes the number of eligible combinations, but payouts, RTP and volatility still depend on the game.

Is Megaways the same as Ways to Win?

Megaways is a variable-reel mechanic that often produces a changing number of ways, while standard Ways systems can use a fixed count.

Do Ways slots need symbols to touch?

Usually symbols need to appear on consecutive reels, but exact adjacency rules depend on the title.
