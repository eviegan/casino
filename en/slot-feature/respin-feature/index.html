Respin Feature Explained

A respin is an additional game event triggered by the slot's rules in which some or all reels spin again, often while selected symbols, Wilds or prize values remain fixed.
Important: Feature names can look similar across providers while using different trigger rules, calculations and eligibility. Always check the exact game's information screen.

Common types & variations

↻

Standard Respin

The reels spin again after a qualifying event.

H

Hold & Respin

Selected symbols lock in place while empty positions respin.

W

Wild Respin

A Wild stays active while remaining reels or positions respin.

3

Resetting Counter

A feature starts with a set number of respins and resets when a qualifying symbol lands.

×

Multiplier Respin

A multiplier can increase as the respin sequence continues.

▦

Grid-Fill Respin

The aim is often to fill more positions before the respin counter reaches zero.

Frequently asked questions

What is a respin in a slot?

A respin is an additional reel or grid result triggered by the game's rules without the player starting a standard new paid spin.

Is a respin the same as a Free Spin?

Not necessarily. Respins often preserve symbols or positions and may affect only part of the grid, while Free Spins usually run as a sequence of bonus spins.

What does it mean when the respin counter resets?

In many hold-and-respin games, landing a new qualifying symbol restores the counter to its starting value.

Can Wilds stay during respins?

Yes. Sticky or persistent Wilds are a common respin mechanic.

Do respins guarantee a win?

No. A respin creates another eligible game event but does not guarantee a winning outcome.
