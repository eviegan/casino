Paylines Explained

A payline is a predefined route across a slot's reels. A winning combination must usually place matching symbols on an active line according to the game's paytable.
Important: Similar feature names can use different mathematics and trigger rules. The exact game information screen should always be treated as the final reference.

Common types & variations

1

Single Payline

Classic slots can use one central horizontal winning line.

5

Small Line Set

Games may use a compact set of straight and diagonal paylines.

40

Fixed 40 Lines

All paylines remain active and the total bet covers the full set.

↔

Left-to-Right

Many slots evaluate matching combinations starting from the leftmost reel.

↔↔

Both Ways

Some games pay combinations from either direction.

$

Line Bet

Older or adjustable-line games can divide the total stake across active paylines.

Frequently asked questions

What is a payline in a slot?

It is a predefined pattern across the reels used to evaluate whether matching symbols form a winning combination.

What does 40 paylines mean?

It means the slot evaluates up to 40 predefined winning line patterns on each spin when all lines are active.

Are more paylines automatically better?

No. More paylines change how combinations are evaluated, but they do not guarantee better results.

Do all slots use paylines?

No. Cluster Pays, Ways to Win and Megaways titles use different systems.

What does left-to-right mean?

It usually means matching symbols must start on the leftmost eligible reel and continue across adjacent reels.
