Why the exact game rules matter

Feature behaviour can affect paylines or ways, symbol substitution, multipliers, respins, Free Spins and the sequence in which wins are calculated. The provider's paytable or game-information screen should therefore be treated as the final reference.

Frequently asked questions

What is what is a cascading slot?

A cascading slot replaces symbols involved in a qualifying win with new symbols, allowing another result to be evaluated from the same paid spin. Providers may call this Cascades, Tumbles or Avalanches.

Does this feature work the same in every slot?

No. Exact rules vary by title and provider, so the game information screen is the final reference.

Does this feature guarantee a win?

No. Slot features change how eligible outcomes are evaluated but do not guarantee a winning result.
