Understand The System

Slot features rarely work alone.

A free-spin feature may use sticky wilds. A cascade may increase a multiplier after each win. A scatter can trigger a bonus game, while the bonus game can contain respins or a jackpot meter. Modern slots are often combinations of several mechanics rather than one isolated feature.

Why Learn Slot Features?

The symbols are only the beginning

Features explain why slots feel different

A classic 20-payline slot, a Megaways title and a cluster-pay game can all be slots while using completely different ways to create wins and bonuses.

The same feature name can hide different rules

“Sticky Wild,” “Free Spins” or “Bonus Buy” describes a broad concept. The exact trigger, multiplier, cost, limits and behavior still depend on the individual game.

FAQ

Slot feature questions

What slot feature should I learn first?

Start with the Symbols Guide, then learn paylines and ways-to-win before moving into bonus features such as free spins, respins and multipliers.

What is the difference between a wild and a scatter?

A wild commonly substitutes for eligible regular symbols, while a scatter commonly triggers free spins or another feature.

What are cascading slots?

After a win, winning symbols are removed and new symbols fall into their positions, potentially creating another win without starting a completely new paid spin.

What is Megaways?

Megaways is a variable-reel system where the number of symbols visible on each reel can change, creating a changing number of possible ways to win.

Are bonus buy and buy feature the same thing?

They are often used for the same general concept: paying a specified amount to enter or trigger a bonus feature directly. Exact terminology and rules vary by provider.

Does every slot have free spins?

No. Some games use respins, bonus games, jackpots, cluster mechanics or other feature structures instead.
