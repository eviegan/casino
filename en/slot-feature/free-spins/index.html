FREESPINS

Free Spins are a common slot bonus feature that awards a defined number of spins without charging the normal stake for each awarded spin. The feature may use the same reels as the base game or introduce enhanced Wilds, multipliers, special symbols or other changed mechanics.

What Are Free Spins?

Free Spins are bonus spins awarded by a slot’s rules in which the player does not place a separate paid stake for each awarded spin. Any wins and feature behaviour are determined by the game’s specific Free Spins rules.

If a game awards 10 Free Spins after the required Scatter symbols land, those 10 spins run under the feature rules without deducting the normal stake again for each spin. The game may also add multipliers or special Wild behaviour during the round.

Important: There is no universal rule shared by every slot. Always check the exact game’s paytable and information screen for trigger requirements, payouts and feature behaviour.

Common types and variations

S

Scatter-Triggered

A required number of Scatter symbols activates the Free Spins round.

↻

Retriggerable

Additional Scatters during the feature can award more Free Spins when the rules allow it.

Multiplier Spins

Wins or special symbols may receive increasing, fixed or collected multipliers during the feature.

W

Enhanced Wilds

Sticky, expanding, walking or multiplier Wilds may appear only or more frequently during Free Spins.

↑

The feature can become stronger as spins continue through collected symbols, levels or expanding mechanics.

Where legally and technically available, some games allow direct entry to a Free Spins or bonus feature for a stated cost.

Frequently asked questions

What are Free Spins in slots?

Free Spins are awarded bonus spins that do not require a separate normal stake for each awarded spin.

How do you trigger Free Spins?

A common trigger is landing a required number of Scatter symbols, but games can use other mechanics as well.

What is a Free Spins retrigger?

A retrigger awards additional Free Spins while the feature is already active, usually after another required symbol combination lands.

Are Free Spins always identical to normal spins?

No. Many games add special Wilds, multipliers, expanding reels or other mechanics during the Free Spins feature.

Do Free Spins guarantee a win?

No. Free Spins provide additional game outcomes under the feature rules but do not guarantee a winning result or profit.
