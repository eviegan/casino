What are Free Spins?

Free Spins are bonus spins awarded by a slot's rules in which the player does not place a separate paid stake for each awarded spin.

The feature may use the same reel layout as the base game or switch to a different set of mechanics for the duration of the round.

How Free Spins are triggered

S

Scatter trigger

A required number of Scatter symbols is one of the most common ways to enter Free Spins.

B

Bonus trigger

Some titles use dedicated Bonus symbols or a collection mechanic.

R

Random award

Certain games can award Free Spins through a random feature or secondary bonus.

BUY

Direct entry

Where permitted, some games offer a paid route into the feature.

Common Free Spins variations

↻

Retriggers

Additional qualifying symbols can award extra spins while the round is active.

×

Multipliers

Wins or special symbols may receive fixed, collected or progressive multipliers.

W

Enhanced Wilds

Sticky, expanding, walking or multiplier Wilds can become more prominent.

↑

Growing feature

Some rounds expand reels, unlock symbols or improve feature conditions as play continues.

What to check before the feature starts

For a clearer understanding of any Free Spins round, check the number of awarded spins, whether retriggers are possible, whether multipliers reset or persist, which Wilds are active and whether the paytable changes during the bonus.

Professional reading tip: “15 Free Spins” does not tell the whole story. The altered rules during those spins often matter more than the raw number of spins.

Frequently asked questions

What are Free Spins in slots?

Free Spins are awarded bonus spins that do not require a separate normal stake for each awarded spin.

What is a Free Spins retrigger?

A retrigger awards additional spins while the feature is already active after another qualifying event occurs.

Do Free Spins always use the same rules as the base game?

No. Many titles introduce special Wilds, multipliers, altered reels or other bonus-only mechanics.

Do Free Spins guarantee a win?

No. They provide additional outcomes under the feature rules but do not guarantee profit.
