Jackpot Features

A slot jackpot is a top-tier prize governed by the game's jackpot rules. Its value may be fixed or progressive, and the trigger may involve a random event, special symbols, a bonus game or another defined mechanic.
Important: Feature names can look similar across providers while using different trigger rules, calculations and eligibility. Always check the exact game's information screen.

Common types & variations

FIX

Fixed Jackpot

The jackpot is defined at a fixed value or fixed multiple under the game rules.

↑

Progressive Jackpot

The displayed prize can increase over time until it is won, then reset according to the system rules.

L

Local Jackpot

A progressive pool may be linked to a limited group of games or one operator environment.

N

Network Jackpot

A wider connected pool can aggregate qualifying activity across multiple participating games or operators.

P

Pick Jackpot

A bonus round can ask the player to reveal or match hidden items to determine a jackpot tier.

4

Multi-Tier Jackpot

Games commonly label several levels such as Mini, Minor, Major and Grand, although names and values vary.

Frequently asked questions

What is a jackpot feature in a slot?

It is a game mechanic connected to a top-tier prize that is awarded when the jackpot's defined trigger conditions are met.

What is the difference between a fixed and progressive jackpot?

A fixed jackpot has a defined prize value under the game rules, while a progressive jackpot can increase until it is won and reset.

What is a network jackpot?

It is a progressive prize pool connected across a wider set of participating games or operators rather than one isolated game instance.

Does a jackpot meter mean the jackpot is due to hit?

No. A displayed value shows the current prize amount, not that a win is due or predictable.

Are jackpot triggers the same in every slot?

No. They can involve random triggers, special symbols, bonus picks, wheels or other game-specific mechanics.
