Sticky Wild Explained

A Sticky Wild is a Wild symbol that remains in position for more than one feature event when the game rules allow it. Sticky Wilds are especially common in respins and Free Spins.

Sticky Wild Explained

A Sticky Wild is a Wild symbol that stays fixed in its reel or grid position for a defined part of a feature rather than disappearing immediately after the result is evaluated.
Important: Similar feature names can use different trigger rules and mathematics. The exact game information screen and paytable remain the final reference.

How Sticky Wilds usually work

1

A Wild lands

The game places a Wild in an eligible position.

2

The feature marks it as sticky

The Wild remains locked instead of leaving on the next feature event.

3

Other reels or symbols continue

New outcomes are evaluated while the Sticky Wild remains available.

4

Additional Sticky Wilds may accumulate

Some features allow several Wilds to remain simultaneously.

5

The sticky state ends

The Wild is removed when the feature, respin sequence or stated duration finishes.

Common Sticky Wild variations

R

Respin Sticky Wild

A Wild remains while other reels respin.

FS

Free Spins Sticky Wild

The Wild can stay for several or all bonus spins.

×

Multiplier Sticky Wild

The Wild retains a multiplier while it remains active.

Growing Sticky Wild

Its size or multiplier can change as the feature develops.

Position-based Wild

A specific grid position remains Wild for the feature.

▥

Reel Sticky Wild

An entire reel or stack can remain Wild under certain rules.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Sticky Wild remain?

Only for the period defined by the game—such as a respin sequence or part of a Free Spins feature.

Is a Sticky Wild always a multiplier Wild?

No. Some Sticky Wilds carry multipliers, while others only provide substitution.

Can more than one Sticky Wild stay at once?

Yes in many features, but the maximum and eligible reels are game-specific.

Does a Sticky Wild guarantee a win?

No. Its position and the surrounding symbols still determine whether an eligible win forms.
