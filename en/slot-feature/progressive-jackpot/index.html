Progressive Jackpot Explained

A progressive jackpot is a jackpot whose value changes according to defined contribution rules until the prize is triggered, awarded and reset to a starting or seed value.
Important: Similar feature names can use different trigger rules and mathematics. The exact game information screen and paytable remain the final reference.

How a progressive jackpot works

1

A starting value is set

The jackpot begins from a predefined seed or reset amount.

2

Qualifying play contributes

A stated portion of qualifying wagers may be allocated to the current jackpot, a future reset pool, or both.

3

The meter increases

The displayed prize can rise as contributions accumulate, subject to the game and network rules.

4

A trigger awards the prize

The jackpot is won only when the defined trigger conditions are met.

5

The system resets

After a verified win, the jackpot returns to its seed value plus any amount reserved for the next cycle.

Common progressive-jackpot structures

L

Local progressive

Linked to one game or a limited operator environment.

N

Network progressive

Pools qualifying activity across a wider set of games or participating operators.

SEED

Seed value

The predefined starting value after a jackpot win.

%

Contribution

The portion of qualifying play allocated under the jackpot rules.

M

Meter

Shows the current prize value; it does not mean the prize is “due”.

T

Trigger

Can be a random event, bonus condition, filled grid or another game-specific rule.

Frequently asked questions

Does a larger progressive meter mean the jackpot is more likely to hit?

No. The displayed amount shows the current prize value, not that a result is due or predictable.

What is a jackpot seed value?

It is the starting amount used after a jackpot resets following a win.

Can part of a bet fund the next jackpot?

Yes. Some systems reserve part of the contribution for the next jackpot cycle.

Are local and network progressive jackpots the same?

No. Local systems use a smaller connected environment, while network systems can pool qualifying activity across a wider group.
