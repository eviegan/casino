How Multipliers Work

A multiplier increases an eligible slot win by a stated factor such as 2×, 3× or 5×. The multiplier can be tied to a Wild, a position, a tumble sequence, a bonus meter or another game-specific rule.

What is a multiplier?

A multiplier increases an eligible win by a stated factor. A 3× multiplier applied to a 10-unit eligible win would produce 30 units, subject to the game's exact rules.

Main multiplier types

WIN

Win multiplier

Applies to a qualifying win or the total win of a spin.

W

Wild multiplier

A Wild can substitute and also multiply the line or combination it helps create.

↑

Progressive multiplier

The multiplier can increase during cascades, respins or a bonus round.

POS

Position multiplier

A specific reel or grid position can carry a multiplier value.

How multiple multipliers combine

Two multiplier values do not always combine in the same way. Some games add them before applying the result, while others multiply the values together or apply them in another game-specific order.

Example: 2× and 3× could become 5× under additive rules, or 6× under multiplicative rules. The paytable decides which interpretation is correct.

What to check in the paytable

1

Scope

Does the multiplier affect one line, one cluster, the whole spin or the entire feature?

2

Duration

Does it apply once, persist for a cascade, or remain through Free Spins?

3

Combination

Are several multipliers added, multiplied or capped?

4

Reset

Does the value reset after each spin, after a losing cascade or only when the feature ends?

Frequently asked questions

What is a slot multiplier?

A multiplier increases an eligible win by a stated factor such as 2×, 3× or 5×.

Do two multipliers always multiply together?

No. Some games add multiplier values while others multiply or apply them under different rules.

Can a Wild have a multiplier?

Yes. Some Wilds substitute for symbols and also apply a multiplier to the winning combination they help create.

Does a multiplier guarantee a win?

No. A multiplier only affects an eligible win when the game rules say it applies.
