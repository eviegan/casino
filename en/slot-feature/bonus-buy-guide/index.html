Bonus Buy is a direct-entry option that lets an eligible player pay a stated multiple of the current stake to enter a bonus feature immediately. The key details are not just the price: the feature type, starting multiplier, RTP and market availability can also change.

What is Bonus Buy?

Bonus Buy is a paid feature-entry mechanic. Instead of waiting for the normal trigger, the player pays a defined cost to activate a particular bonus mode directly.

Similar feature names can use different mathematics, trigger rules and prices. The exact game information screen should always be treated as the final reference.

Common types & variations

80×

Standard Buy

A fixed multiple of the current bet can unlock one defined feature.

200×

Premium Buy

A more expensive option may unlock a stronger feature or different starting conditions.

Tier

Tiered Buy

Several purchase levels can correspond to different scatter counts or starting multipliers.

RTP

Mode RTP

The theoretical RTP can differ between normal play and a bought feature.

↑

Ante / Enhanced Chance

Some games increase the stake to improve trigger frequency instead of guaranteeing entry.

🌐

Market Control

Bonus Buy may be disabled in jurisdictions where the mechanic is not permitted.

What to check before using a Bonus Buy

A professional way to read a Bonus Buy screen is to separate the price from the feature being purchased. A higher multiple does not automatically mean a better expected result.

1

Cost multiple

Confirm whether the purchase is 50×, 80×, 100×, 200× or another amount of the current stake.

2

Feature entered

Check whether the purchase enters Free Spins, Super Free Spins, a respin feature or another mode.

3

RTP / volatility

Where the game publishes mode-specific data, compare it with the base game rather than assuming they are identical.

4

Availability

Operator and jurisdiction settings can determine whether the feature is available at all.

Remember: paying more guarantees only access to the stated feature, not a profitable outcome.

Frequently asked questions

What is Bonus Buy?

It is a paid option that activates a defined slot bonus directly rather than waiting for the natural trigger.

How much does a Bonus Buy cost?

There is no universal cost. Prices are commonly expressed as a multiple of the current bet and vary by feature.

Does Bonus Buy have the same RTP as base play?

Not always. Some titles publish different theoretical return characteristics for different modes.

Is Bonus Buy available in every country?

No. Jurisdictional rules, operator settings and certified versions can disable the mechanic.

Does paying more guarantee a profit?

No. A higher price can buy access to a different feature configuration, not a guaranteed positive result.
