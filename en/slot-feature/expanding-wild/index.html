What is an Expanding Wild?

An Expanding Wild is a Wild symbol that grows beyond its original position to cover multiple symbol spaces—commonly an entire reel—when the game's rules activate the feature.

Expansion does not have one universal pattern. It may happen vertically, horizontally, during a respin, only in Free Spins or under another title-specific condition.

Common Expanding Wild variations

↕

Full-reel Wild

A Wild grows vertically to cover every eligible position on its reel.

↔

Horizontal expansion

Less common designs can expand across rows or adjacent positions.

↻

Expansion + respin

The expanded Wild can remain while the game awards another evaluation.

FS

Free Spins expansion

Wilds may expand only, or more frequently, during a Free Spins feature.

Frequently asked questions

What is an Expanding Wild?

It is a Wild symbol that grows to cover more than its original symbol position, often an entire reel.

Does every Expanding Wild cover a full reel?

No. Full-reel expansion is common, but some games use different directions, grid areas or feature-specific rules.

Can an Expanding Wild trigger a respin?

Yes. Some titles combine expansion with a respin or keep the expanded Wild active during a feature.

Can Expanding Wilds appear in Free Spins?

Yes. Many slots use expanding Wilds as an enhanced mechanic during Free Spins.
