What a scatter symbol is

A scatter is a special slot symbol whose behavior is defined by the game's paytable. Unlike standard payline symbols, scatters often count regardless of their exact position on the reels.

Scatters are most commonly associated with free spins or bonus features, but some games also award direct scatter payouts or use them as part of jackpot mechanics.

How scatter symbols can work

FS

Free Spins

A required number of scatters may trigger a free-spin round.

B

Bonus Game

Scatters can open wheels, picks or other feature games.

$

Direct Payout

Some titles also pay a prize for landing enough scatter symbols.

R

Retrigger

Additional scatters inside a feature may award more free spins.

Why the paytable matters

Three scatters is common, but it is not a universal standard. The game's paytable should tell you:

  • how many scatters are required;
  • whether they can land on all reels;
  • whether they award a direct payout;
  • whether they retrigger a feature;
  • whether special scatter values apply.
Best reference: Use the actual game-information screen rather than assuming every slot follows the same scatter rules.

Frequently asked questions

How many scatters do you need?

Three is common, but the exact trigger requirement depends on the individual game.

Does a scatter have to land on a payline?

Often no, but the game rules determine how the symbol is evaluated.

Can a scatter also be a wild?

Some games use hybrid symbols, but standard scatter and wild symbols usually have separate roles.

Do all slots have scatters?

No. Some games use different mechanisms for triggering bonuses.

Can scatters retrigger free spins?

Yes in some games, but the exact retrigger rule varies by title.
