What a multiplier means

A multiplier increases an eligible game value by a stated factor such as ×2, ×3, ×5, ×10 or more.

If an eligible win is $10 and a ×5 multiplier applies, the resulting value would usually become $50 before any other limits or rules are applied.

Can multiple multipliers combine?

Yes, but different games use different rules. Two multiplier values might be:

  • added together;
  • multiplied together;
  • applied separately;
  • limited to the highest value only.
Example: ×2 and ×3 could equal ×5 under additive rules or ×6 under multiplicative rules. The paytable decides which is correct.

Multipliers in cascading and tumbling slots

Cascade slots often increase the multiplier after consecutive wins within the same paid spin.

A feature might progress from ×1 to ×2, then ×3, ×4 and higher until no further cascade occurs. Some games reset the multiplier after every paid spin, while others carry values into free spins.

Maximum multipliers and maximum-win rules

A slot can advertise a very large possible multiplier while still imposing a maximum overall payout. These are separate rules.

When evaluating a multiplier feature, check:

  • maximum multiplier;
  • maximum game payout;
  • whether multipliers stack;
  • whether the multiplier resets;
  • whether different rules apply in free spins.

Frequently asked questions

What does ×10 mean in a slot?

It usually means the eligible value is multiplied by ten, subject to the game's rules.

Can multiplier wilds stack?

Some games allow multiple multiplier wilds to combine, while others use different calculations.

Are multipliers guaranteed in free spins?

No. Their appearance and behavior depend on the individual game.

Is a multiplier the same as a jackpot?

No. A multiplier increases an eligible value; a jackpot is a designated prize with its own trigger rules.

Can multipliers reset?

Yes. Many games reset multipliers after a spin, cascade sequence or bonus round.
