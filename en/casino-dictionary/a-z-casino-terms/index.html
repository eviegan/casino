Casino terminology without the jargon

Casino terminology covers several different areas at once: game mathematics, slot mechanics, table-game rules, bonuses, payments, account security and responsible-gambling tools. Learning the language makes it much easier to understand what a game actually does and what promotional terms really mean.

For example, RTP tells you about theoretical long-term return, while volatility describes how payouts are distributed. A scatter might trigger free spins, while a wild usually helps form winning combinations. These terms are related, but they are not interchangeable.

Important: Definitions can vary slightly between providers, jurisdictions and individual games. The game's own paytable, help screen and terms should always be treated as the final authority.

Core casino terms you will see everywhere

RTP

RTP

Return to Player: a theoretical percentage describing how much of all eligible wagers a game is designed to return over a very large number of rounds.

RNG

Random Number Generator

The mechanism used by many digital casino games to generate unpredictable outcomes.

VOL

Volatility

A practical description of the theoretical payout pattern of a game, commonly labelled low, medium or high.

VAR

Variance

A statistical concept describing how widely results can spread around an expected average.

BET

Stake / Wager

The amount placed at risk on a round, spin, hand or betting outcome.

HE

House Edge

The mathematical advantage built into a casino game over sufficiently long play.

Slot terminology: reels, symbols and features

Modern slots use a wide variety of layouts. Some still use traditional paylines, while others use ways-to-win systems, cluster pays, cascades, expanding grids or feature-specific mechanics.

W

Wild

A special symbol that can substitute for selected regular symbols. Some wilds also expand, stick, move or carry multipliers.

S

Scatter

A special symbol commonly associated with free spins or bonus triggers, often without needing to land on a standard payline.

×

Multiplier

A mechanic that increases an eligible value by a stated factor such as ×2, ×5 or ×10.

JP

Jackpot

A designated prize that may be fixed, random or progressive.

PT

Paytable

The game-information screen that explains symbol values, winning rules, bonus triggers and special mechanics.

FS

Free Spins

A feature awarding slot rounds without the usual cash stake for each awarded spin, according to the game's rules.

Other common terms include cascades, tumbles, cluster pays, ways to win, expanding reels, respins and feature buy.

Bonus and promotion terminology

Casino bonuses can be more confusing than the games themselves because promotional value depends heavily on the terms.

WR

Wagering Requirement

The amount of eligible wagering that may be required before certain bonus funds or related winnings become withdrawable.

GC

Game Contribution

The percentage at which a particular game contributes toward a wagering requirement.

MB

Maximum Bet

The largest wager allowed while a promotion or bonus balance is active.

EXP

Expiry

The time limit for claiming, using or completing the conditions of a promotion.

A promotion that says “100% bonus” does not by itself tell you how valuable the offer is. Wagering requirements, eligible games, maximum bets, withdrawal caps and expiry rules can all materially change how the bonus works.

How to use this dictionary while playing

Before trying an unfamiliar game, open its information screen and look for five things: RTP, volatility, winning system, special symbols and feature rules. For a bonus, check wagering requirements, game contribution, maximum bet, expiry and withdrawal restrictions.

Understanding the terminology does not predict results, but it helps you understand what you are agreeing to and how a game or promotion is designed.

How to use casino terminology professionally

A dictionary is most useful when the reader knows which terms are universal concepts and which depend on the exact game, provider or jurisdiction.

1

Separate mathematics from mechanics: RTP, house edge and volatility describe mathematical properties; wilds, scatters and bonus rounds describe game features.

2

Check the game’s own rules: a familiar term can behave differently between titles.

3

Use regulator definitions for formal terms: bonus balance, deposit balance and wagering requirement can have precise regulatory meanings.

4

Do not treat terminology as prediction: understanding RTP, RNG or volatility does not predict the next result.

Frequently asked questions

What does RTP mean?

RTP means Return to Player. It describes a game's theoretical long-run return percentage, not a guaranteed return for one session.

What does RNG mean?

RNG means Random Number Generator, a mechanism used to generate unpredictable digital game outcomes.

Are volatility and variance the same?

They are closely related, but variance is the formal statistical concept while volatility is the more practical player-facing description.

What is the difference between a wild and a scatter?

A wild usually helps form winning combinations, while a scatter commonly triggers free spins or another feature.

Where should I check a game's exact rules?

Use the game's own information, help or paytable screen because mechanics can vary between titles.
