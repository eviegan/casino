Wilds are some of the most flexible symbols in slot design: they can substitute, expand, stay in place, move across reels or multiply a win.

What a wild symbol does

A standard wild commonly substitutes for selected regular symbols to help form a qualifying winning combination.

Wilds are one of the most flexible slot mechanics because providers often enhance them with extra behavior such as expansion, movement, persistence or multipliers.

Common types of wild

Several wild symbols appear together on the same reel.

A simple substitution example

Imagine a winning line displays K – K – WILD – K – K. If the wild is allowed to substitute for the King symbol, the game may evaluate that line as five Kings.

The actual payout depends on the paytable, line value and whether the game uses paylines, ways-to-win, clusters or another winning system.

What wilds cannot always replace

Wild symbols frequently have exclusions. Depending on the title, they may not replace:

  • scatter symbols;
  • bonus symbols;
  • jackpot symbols;
  • collect symbols;
  • special feature icons.

That is why “wild substitutes for all symbols” should never be assumed unless the game explicitly says so.

Wilds inside bonus features

Many games make wilds more powerful during free spins. They may become sticky, carry multipliers, expand to full reels or accumulate during the feature.

This can dramatically change the short-term payout pattern of the bonus round compared with the base game.

How to read wild-symbol rules professionally

“Wild” is a category of feature, not one universal rule. The exact substitution and bonus behavior should always come from the game’s own paytable.

1

Check substitutions: a wild may exclude scatters, bonus symbols, jackpots or other special icons.

2

Check persistence: sticky, walking and expanding wilds can behave very differently across titles.

3

Check multiplier logic: multiplier wilds can add, multiply or apply under game-specific rules.

4

Check feature-specific behavior: a wild can work differently in base play and free spins.

Frequently asked questions

Can a wild replace a scatter?

Usually not, although the exact substitution rules depend on the game.

What is a sticky wild?

A sticky wild remains in position for multiple eligible spins or stages of a feature.

What is a multiplier wild?

It is a wild that can both substitute and multiply an eligible winning combination.

Does landing a wild guarantee a win?

No. The final symbol arrangement still has to satisfy the game's winning rules.

Can wilds behave differently in free spins?

Yes. Many games enhance wild behavior during bonus features.
