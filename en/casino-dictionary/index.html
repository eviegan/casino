Why This Dictionary?

Casino terms should explain more than jargon

Many terms sound similar

RTP, house edge, variance and volatility all describe different parts of casino mathematics. Wilds and scatters are both special slot symbols but usually perform different functions. Bonuses and cashback are both rewards but are often calculated differently.

Context makes the definition useful

That is why the articles in this dictionary include practical examples, common misconceptions, comparison tables, external references and related guides instead of only one-sentence definitions.

FAQ

Casino dictionary questions

What casino term should I learn first?

RTP is a strong starting point because it introduces theoretical return. After that, volatility, RNG and house edge help build the mathematical picture.

Are RTP and house edge the same thing?

They describe opposite sides of the same long-run relationship in a simplified model. RTP represents theoretical return to players, while house edge represents the casino's theoretical advantage.

What is the difference between a wild and a scatter?

A wild usually substitutes for eligible symbols to help form wins. A scatter commonly triggers free spins or another bonus feature.

What is the difference between bonus and cashback?

Bonuses are often credited upfront or after a qualifying action, while cashback is commonly calculated after eligible play based on losses, wagering or another formula.

Why are casino terms different between games?

Developers can use their own rules and feature designs. A general dictionary explains the concept, but the game's paytable or information screen remains the final reference.

What does wagering mean?

Wagering refers to the value of bets placed. It is different from deposits, withdrawals and net losses.
