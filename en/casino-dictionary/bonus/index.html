What is a casino bonus?

A casino bonus is a promotional benefit offered under specific terms. It may be delivered as bonus balance, free spins, cashback, a deposit match, a reward ticket or another promotional format.

Bonuses are not all cash-equivalent. Some are locked until wagering conditions are completed, some have maximum withdrawal limits, and some expire if not used within a stated period.

Why the biggest headline bonus is not always the best

A “200% bonus” can look more attractive than a “100% bonus,” but that comparison is incomplete without checking:

  • wagering requirement;
  • maximum bonus amount;
  • minimum deposit;
  • game contribution;
  • maximum bet while wagering;
  • expiry period;
  • maximum cashout or withdrawal restrictions.

A smaller bonus with simpler terms can sometimes be more flexible than a larger headline offer.

How wagering requirements affect bonuses

If a bonus requires 30× wagering, the player must complete the required eligible turnover before withdrawal conditions are met.

Watch the formula: “30× bonus” and “30× deposit + bonus” are very different requirements. Always identify what amount the multiplier applies to.

Anatomy of a casino bonus: headline vs reality

Imagine two offers. Casino A advertises 200% up to $200 with a demanding playthrough and short expiry. Casino B advertises 100% up to $100 with simpler conditions. The first headline is bigger, but that does not automatically make it the more usable promotion.

A useful way to read a bonus is to separate it into three layers: qualification (what you must do to receive it), usage (how you may play with it), and withdrawal (what must happen before eligible winnings can be withdrawn).

Cashable, sticky and no-wagering bonuses

Not every bonus balance behaves the same way. A cashable bonus may become withdrawable after its conditions are completed. A “sticky” or non-cashable bonus may be removed at withdrawal while only eligible winnings remain. A no-wagering offer can remove the traditional playthrough requirement, but other restrictions may still apply.

These differences are exactly why comparing bonuses only by percentage can be misleading.

Frequently asked questions

Are casino bonuses free money?

Not necessarily. Promotional funds usually have terms, restrictions or wagering conditions.

What is a deposit match bonus?

It is a bonus calculated as a percentage of an eligible deposit, often up to a maximum amount.

Can bonus funds expire?

Yes. Many promotions have claim, activation or usage deadlines.

Do free spins have wagering requirements?

Sometimes. The free spins themselves may be free, but resulting winnings can have conditions.

Why should I read bonus terms?

Because headline percentages do not show wagering, game restrictions, maximum bets or cashout rules.
