Before making a payment

Check the operator’s authorization where applicable, use the official website, read cashier rules and verify that the payment page belongs to the expected service.

Protect your account

Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication when available, and secure the email and phone number connected to payment approvals.

Verify payment details

Confirm the recipient, amount and payment method before approval. Do not rely on payment instructions sent by strangers through social media or messaging apps.

Phishing and fake support

Fraudsters may imitate casino support, banks or payment providers. Legitimate support should not need your password or one-time banking authentication code.

Keep transaction records

Receipts, transaction IDs, screenshots of statuses and support case numbers can help trace disputes.

Payment red flags

Unexpected recipient changes, pressure to act immediately, personal-account transfers, requests to pay to unlock winnings and demands for secret credentials should be treated cautiously.

Professional payment-security checklist

Use a repeatable verification routine before every deposit or withdrawal so unfamiliar requests are easier to spot.

1

Verify the channel: access the cashier through the official site rather than links sent by strangers.

2

Check recipient details: compare the amount and payee information before approving a transfer.

3

Protect authentication: never share passwords, recovery codes or one-time banking codes with support.

4

Keep evidence: save receipts, transaction IDs and support case numbers until the issue is fully resolved.

Frequently asked questions

Is HTTPS enough to prove a casino is safe?

No. HTTPS protects the connection but does not prove operator authorization or reliability.

Should support ask for my banking code?

Do not share passwords or one-time banking authentication codes with support agents.

Important: Payment availability and regulatory requirements can change. Verify current operator and payment rules before moving money.
