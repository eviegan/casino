How crypto payments work

A crypto transfer sends digital assets between blockchain addresses. The user normally selects a network, verifies the destination and approves the transaction from a wallet or exchange.

Brazilian regulatory context

Technical availability does not mean a payment method is permitted in a regulated market. For Brazilian federally authorized fixed-odds betting, players should check current permitted payment rules rather than assuming an offshore crypto option follows them.

Networks and addresses

Using the wrong network or destination address can result in permanent loss. Blockchain transfers often lack the reversal mechanisms available in traditional payment systems.

Price volatility

Cryptocurrency values can change between deposit and withdrawal, creating gains or losses in fiat value independently of gambling results.

Fees and confirmations

Networks can charge transaction fees and require confirmations before a transfer is considered final. Cost and speed vary.

Key risks

Wallet security, phishing, address errors, exchange restrictions, price volatility and local regulatory requirements all matter. Crypto is not automatically anonymous or safer.

Frequently asked questions

Does crypto make a casino legal?

No. Payment technology does not determine whether an operator is authorized.

Can crypto transfers be reversed?

Blockchain transfers are often difficult or impossible to reverse after confirmation.

Important: Payment availability and regulatory requirements can change. Verify current operator and payment rules before moving money.
