Deposit is pending

Check whether the bank shows the transfer as completed, whether the amount and recipient are correct, and whether the casino cashier has a matching transaction record.

Bank paid but balance is missing

Keep the receipt and transaction ID. Do not immediately pay again. Contact official support so the original transfer can be traced.

Payment is rejected

Possible causes include transaction limits, unsupported methods, authentication failure, account-ownership rules, incorrect details or risk controls.

Withdrawal is taking too long

Check the withdrawal status, KYC completion, registered payment details and published processing rules. Separate operator processing from bank transfer time.

Account or KYC mismatch

Names, identity information or payment ownership that do not match can stop a transaction. Correct information through the official account process rather than creating duplicate accounts.

Contacting support effectively

Provide the transaction ID, amount, timestamp, payment method and a clear description. Never provide passwords or one-time authentication codes.

When to investigate further

Repeatedly changing requirements, unofficial payment demands, unexplained missing funds or unverifiable authorization are reasons to document the issue and consider appropriate complaint channels.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do when a PIX deposit is missing?

Check the bank and cashier records, save the receipt and contact official support before sending another payment.

Does a payment problem automatically mean a scam?

No. Technical and verification problems happen, but repeated unexplained issues and unofficial payment demands are warning signs.

Important: Payment availability and regulatory requirements can change. Verify current operator and payment rules before moving money.
