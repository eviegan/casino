FAQ

Casino technology questions

What technology powers online casino games?

Modern casinos commonly use HTML5 game clients, backend servers, APIs and provider platforms.

How do live dealer games work?

Physical tables are filmed in real time while software handles betting windows, result capture and digital settlement.

Why is HTML5 important?

HTML5 allows games to run directly in modern browsers across desktop and mobile without old plug-ins.

How is AI used in online casinos?

AI can support fraud detection, personalization, customer service and responsible-gambling monitoring.

What is game fairness technology?

It includes RNG testing, certification, logging and technical controls that help verify game behavior.

Will VR replace normal online casinos?

VR may expand immersive options, but traditional browser and mobile casino formats are likely to remain important.
