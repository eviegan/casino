What actually happens in an RNG game?

The RNG produces values that the game maps to allowed outcomes according to its rules and mathematics. In an RNG roulette game, for example, the displayed wheel animation represents a software-determined result.

Evolution states that its RNG offerings are tested and certified by independent accredited testing facilities for regulated markets.

What happens in a live dealer game?

A live dealer physically deals cards, spins a wheel or operates other equipment. Video is streamed to the player while software captures the result and settles digital bets.

Evolution describes its studios as advanced TV/film-style environments using multi-camera, audio and IT systems to broadcast games in real time.

Is one more fair than the other?

“Live” does not automatically mean fairer, and “RNG” does not automatically mean less trustworthy. Fairness depends on licensing, testing, game rules, software integrity, operational controls and jurisdiction.

Important: Neither system makes outcomes predictable. A live roulette wheel is not easier to forecast simply because it is physical.

Which feels more transparent?

Live dealer can feel more visually transparent because players watch the physical action. RNG is faster and more scalable. The stronger comparison is not “real vs fake,” but “physical outcome streamed digitally vs software-generated outcome displayed digitally.”

Frequently asked questions

What is an RNG game?

A digital casino game where software generates the outcome according to the game's approved mathematics.

What makes a live dealer game live?

A human dealer operates physical gaming equipment while the game is streamed in real time.

Is live dealer more random?

Not inherently. The two formats use different outcome-generation methods.

Can RNG games be tested?

Yes. Regulated markets commonly require approved testing and certification.

Are live dealer games slower?

Usually yes because they follow real-time table actions and betting windows.
