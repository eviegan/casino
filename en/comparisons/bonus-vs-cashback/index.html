Bonuses and cashback can both add promotional value, but they usually reward different moments in the player journey. A bonus often arrives before or during play; cashback is commonly calculated after qualifying activity.

Two offers that look equal—but are not

Offer A gives a $100 deposit bonus with a 20× wagering requirement. Offer B gives 10% weekly cashback on eligible net losses up to $100. Both can theoretically produce $100 of promotional value, but the conditions and timing are completely different.

The bonus gives value upfront but may require substantial eligible turnover. Cashback gives no immediate extra balance, but may return part of a qualifying loss later.

Why bonus rules are becoming a bigger topic

Promotional rules can change by jurisdiction. In Great Britain, for example, the Gambling Commission introduced a maximum 10× wagering requirement for bonus funds effective 19 January 2026, alongside other changes intended to make promotions clearer and safer.

That does not mean every country uses the same limit. It shows why promotion terms should be read in the context of the player's market.

Which is simpler to understand?

Often cashback is conceptually simpler because it can be expressed as “X% of an eligible calculation.” But the calculation itself may still be complicated. Bonuses can be more flexible and varied, but they often have more moving parts.

Frequently asked questions

Is cashback a type of bonus?

It is a promotional reward, but it is usually calculated from qualifying activity after play rather than credited upfront.

Can cashback have wagering requirements?

Yes. Cashback can be cash, bonus balance or another reward type depending on the program.

Which is better: bonus or cashback?

There is no universal answer; compare conditions, caps, contribution rules and timing.

Does cashback always use net losses?

No. Some programs use wagering, rake or another calculation.

Can a bonus be more valuable than cashback?

Yes, but headline value alone does not show how easy the reward is to use or withdraw.
