BGaming trademark mechanics

BGaming’s official catalogue highlights proprietary mechanics including TRUEWAYS™, MultiDice X™, MergeUP™ and SpinUp™. These systems change how symbols, clusters, dice or feature modes behave, so the paytable remains essential for understanding each title.

RTP and volatility

BGaming publishes RTP and volatility information for many titles in its official catalogue. RTP is a theoretical long-run percentage rather than a prediction for a session. A higher listed RTP does not mean a game is due to pay, and volatility can materially change how results are distributed.

Fairness and game information

Before playing, check the exact game’s RTP, volatility, paytable, maximum win, feature rules and total stake. Where a title includes special verification or fairness information, use the provider’s documentation for the exact implementation.
