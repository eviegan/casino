What games does NetEnt make?

NetEnt’s current catalogue is strongly slot-led, with individual titles using paylines, Avalanche-style mechanics, feature buys where permitted, free spins, jackpots and other game-specific systems.

RTP, volatility and exact game versions

NetEnt publishes title-level RTP and game data on many official game pages. RTP is a theoretical long-run percentage and does not predict a short session. Always check the exact title and market version because game settings and availability can differ.

NetEnt and the Evolution group

NetEnt is part of the wider Evolution portfolio. That relationship does not make NetEnt a casino operator: it remains a content brand supplying games through casino platforms.
