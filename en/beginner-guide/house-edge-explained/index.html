House edge sounds technical, but the core idea is simple: it is the mathematical advantage built into a casino game over the long term.

What is the house edge?

The house edge is the theoretical long-term mathematical advantage built into a casino game in favor of the operator.

A 3% house edge means that, over a very large number of wagers, the game is designed to retain about 3 units for every 100 units wagered. Short-term results can be very different.

How does house edge work?

The house edge comes from game rules, payout tables and probabilities. It does not mean the casino wins every round. It means the mathematics create an expected advantage over time.

Expected value

The house edge is an average expectation over many rounds, not a fixed fee removed from every wager.

Why can the house edge change?

R

Different rules

Small changes to rules can alter the mathematical advantage.

P

Payout tables

Lower or higher payouts change the expected value of a game.

S

Strategy decisions

In decision-based games, player choices can affect the effective edge.

V

Game versions

Different versions of a similar title may use different mathematics.

How should beginners use house edge?

Use it as a comparison tool, not as a prediction. Review the exact rules, game version and payout information. A lower house edge may be mathematically preferable, but it does not guarantee a better result in a short session.

No guaranteed outcomes

Even a game with a low house edge can produce losses. Set limits and avoid chasing previous results.

Frequently asked questions

Is a lower house edge better for players?

Mathematically, a lower house edge means a smaller long-term advantage for the casino, assuming comparable conditions.

Does house edge mean the casino wins every round?

No. Players can win individual rounds and sessions, but the long-term expected value favors the casino.

Can player decisions change house edge?

In some games, yes. Rules and decisions can change the effective mathematical advantage.

Is house edge the same as RTP?

They are related but opposite perspectives. RTP describes theoretical return to players, while house edge describes the casino advantage.

Can a game have zero house edge?

Casino games are generally designed with some mathematical advantage or revenue mechanism, though special promotions may temporarily change conditions.
