FAQ

Beginner questions

What should I learn first about online casinos?

Start with what an online casino is and how the platform works. Then move into RTP, RNG, house edge and volatility.

What does RTP mean?

RTP means Return to Player. It describes a game's theoretical long-term return across a very large amount of play.

What is RNG?

RNG means Random Number Generator. It is used by digital casino games to generate unpredictable outcomes according to the game's mathematics.

What is house edge?

House edge is the casino's theoretical long-term mathematical advantage. The exact percentage varies by game and sometimes by bet.

Why should beginners learn responsible gambling early?

Understanding spending limits, session time and bankroll control helps keep gambling separate from essential money and other commitments.
