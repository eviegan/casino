Brazilian players often see a mix of English casino vocabulary and Portuguese payment or account terms. This guide explains the words clearly so beginners can understand game pages, bonuses, payments and account messages.

Core casino terms

Bet / wager

The amount placed on a game outcome.

Balance

The amount shown in a player’s account. It may be divided into cash balance and bonus balance.

Stake

Another word for the amount risked on a game round.

Payout

The amount returned after a winning result, according to the game rules.

Session

The period of time a player spends using the platform or playing games.

Game mathematics

RTP

Return to Player is a theoretical long-term percentage built into a game’s mathematics. It does not predict a short session.

House edge

The mathematical advantage held by the casino over the long term.

Volatility

A description of how often and how strongly a game may produce wins or losses.

Variance

A statistical way of describing how far results may move away from the average.

Odds

A way of expressing the likelihood of an outcome and sometimes the amount that may be returned.

Important

RTP, odds and volatility describe game mathematics. They do not guarantee that an individual player will win.

Common slot terms

W

Wild

A symbol that may substitute for other symbols according to the game rules.

S

Scatter

A symbol that may trigger a feature or award when enough appear.

M

Multiplier

A feature that increases a payout by a stated factor.

F

Free spins

Additional game rounds awarded under specified conditions.

P

Payline

A pattern across the reels used to evaluate winning symbol combinations.

J

Jackpot

A larger prize that may be fixed or progressive, depending on the game.

Bonus and promotion terms

Welcome bonus

A promotion generally offered to eligible new users under stated terms.

Deposit match

A bonus calculated as a percentage of an eligible deposit, subject to limits and conditions.

Wagering requirement

The amount that bonus-related funds may need to be played through before withdrawal is permitted.

Maximum bet

The largest wager allowed while using a bonus or promotion.

Expiry date

The deadline for activating or completing a promotion.

Cashback

A promotion that may return part of qualifying losses under specific terms.

Payment and PIX terms Brazilian players may see

PIX

Brazil’s instant-payment system. Availability, limits and processing rules depend on the operator and payment provider.

Deposit

Funds added to an online casino account through a supported method.

Withdrawal / saque

A request to transfer eligible funds out of the account.

Processing time

The period between submitting a transaction and its completion.

Pending

A status showing that a request is still being reviewed or processed.

Minimum / maximum

The smallest or largest amount allowed for a deposit, withdrawal or transaction.

Account and verification terms

K

KYC

“Know Your Customer” checks used to confirm identity, age and other account information.

CPF

CPF

A Brazilian taxpayer identification number that may be requested where legally and operationally required.

2F

Two-factor authentication

An extra login step using a code, device or authentication application.

SE

Self-exclusion

A responsible-gambling control that restricts access for a chosen period or under specific rules.

Safety and responsible-gambling terms

Licensed operator

An operator that claims authorization from a named regulator. Players should verify the claim independently.

Encryption

Technology used to protect information transmitted between a user and a website.

Deposit limit

A control that restricts how much can be deposited during a selected period.

Reality check

A time reminder showing how long a player has been active.

Cooling-off period

A temporary break during which account access or gambling activity may be restricted.

Responsible gambling

Tools and practices intended to help users control time and spending and reduce harm.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Brazilian players see both English and Portuguese casino terms?

Many platforms, games and providers use English terminology, while payments and account processes may also use Portuguese terms.

What does RTP mean?

RTP means Return to Player. It is a theoretical long-term percentage and does not predict short-term results.

What does saque mean?

Saque is the Portuguese word commonly used for a withdrawal from an account.

What is KYC?

KYC means Know Your Customer. It refers to identity and account checks used by operators and payment services.

Does a bonus always mean free money?

No. Bonuses normally include eligibility rules, wagering requirements, limits and expiry conditions.
